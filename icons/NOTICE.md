# Icon notice

These generic media-control SVGs and PNG renditions are project-created geometry
for Pear Desktop Connector, distributed under the repository's MIT license.
They replace the inherited bitmap artwork; no Google, YouTube, Pear, or Elgato
logo, photograph, icon font, or third-party icon set is included.

Editable geometry and generation: `scripts/generate-icons.py` with Python 3 and
Inkscape 1.4.4. PNGs are 72×72 and 144×144, except the category icon at 28×28
and 56×56; SVG viewBox is 144×144. Inkscape is an
authoring tool and is not shipped with the plugin. State pairs share geometry
with white/gray/mint colors; Repeat also has different center symbols.
